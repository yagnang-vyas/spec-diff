Playground

Paste or load two OpenAPI 3.0.x / 3.1.x documents (YAML or JSON) and compare them with the SpecDiff engine. Everything runs in this page: your documents are not uploaded, stored or put in the URL.

The documents changed after this comparison. Press Compare to update the results.

Uses the SpecDiff comparison engine compiled for the browser. Limits: OpenAPI 3.0.x/3.1.x only, up to 1 MB per document, and $refs must point inside the document. See the CLI vs. Playground notes.
